- `Topic::setKeyFilter` and `Topic::setSampleFilter` now throw `std::logic_error` when the topic already created its
  reader or writer, and are no longer `noexcept`. Registering a filter at that point was never safe: the topic shares
  its filters with its reader and writer, and the Ice dispatch threads read them while the application registers a
  new one. Register all filters right after constructing the topic.
